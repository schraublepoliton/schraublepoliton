About this audit

This document summarizes the scope, methodology and contributors for the independent technical and design audit of Escape from Tarkov.

View Findings Get in touch

Scope & methodology

The audit focuses on core systems that materially affect player experience: networking, performance, progression, economy and anti-cheat. Analysis combines hands-on testing, community reports and published patch notes.

Data sources

  • Community bug reports and reproducible cases
  • On‑device profiling and performance traces
  • Developer patch notes and release history

Deliverables

  • Executive summary
  • Prioritized findings with remediation guidance
  • Design recommendations for longer‑term stability

Contributors

This is a community-driven, independent analysis. If you'd like to contribute data or collaborate, use the contact page.
