Summary

The in-game economy demonstrates high volatility and friction that penalizes casual players. A combination of market regulation, vendor restocks and economic sinks can stabilise value flows.

Key issues

  • High item price volatility on the flea market.
  • Wipe model creates sudden resets that harm long-term retention.
  • Premium containers and edition-based advantages skew early progression.

Recommendations

  • Introduce vendor restock algorithms tied to player demand.
  • Add inflation controls and currency sinks to stabilise price drift.
  • Rework premium item gating to focus on convenience rather than competitive advantage.
