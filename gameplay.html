Design observations

Core gameplay systems are deep and engaging but suffer from steep learning curves, grind-heavy progression, and occasional balance pitfalls. Targeted design changes can preserve depth while reducing unnecessary friction.

Progression & quests

Quests provide long-term goals but often require excessive repetition. Consider redesigned quest chains with intermediate rewards, clearer goals and alternative completion paths.

Balance & matchmaking

Spawn fairness and matchmaking (for new vs veteran players) should be improved to reduce early-session frustration. Introduce safer starter areas and better onboarding for new accounts.

Player recommendations

  • Implement a guided tutorial for first 10 hours of play.
  • Reduce grind pressure for early-tier progression tasks.
  • Surface metrics to players (e.g., quest progress, expected completion time).
