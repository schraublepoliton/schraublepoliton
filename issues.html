Executive summary

The product exhibits several high-severity issues that materially degrade player experience: network synchronization, exploit prevalence, and inconsistent audio/visual feedback. Below are the findings, impact assessment and recommended remediation steps.

1. Network synchronization (Desync)

Overview: Clients and server disagree about entity positions in non-trivial scenarios, resulting in 'phantom' hits or misses.

  • Impact: High — causes player frustration, perceived unfairness.
  • Recommendation: Add authoritative reconciliation logs, increase tick rate for critical interactions, and deploy server-side hit validation telemetry.
  • Priority: P0 — Immediate investigation required.

2. Anti-cheat & exploit mitigation

Overview: Reports indicate persistent undetected cheats and account abuse.

  • Impact: High — undermines competitive integrity and player retention.
  • Recommendation: Harden server-side validation, implement heuristic detection pipelines, and accelerate ban enforcement with automated account flagging.
  • Priority: P0 — short-term enforcement + medium-term architecture improvements.

Audio & perception

Inaccurate spatial audio and inconsistent cueing reduce tactical reliability. Improve audio occlusion, use higher-quality HRTF and validate vertical positioning of sources.

AI behavior

NPCs occasionally ignore obvious threats or path through geometry. Increase QA coverage and add deterministic AI unit tests.

Bug regression cadence

Frequent regressions introduced by releases. Adopt stronger CI, automated regression tests, and staged rollouts.
